# Ancient Magic — Glossary

Canonical terms for mod design and development. Definitions are player-facing concepts only — no file paths or implementation detail. See [docs/README.md](docs/README.md) for documentation conventions.

## Core magic

**Mana**:
A fundamental force and raw expression of potential that mages channel to bend reality; spent when casting spells and replenished over time.
_Avoid_: Magic Power (used in some loc keys)

**Mana Affinity**:
Innate talent for manipulating arcane forces, often congenital and passed through a dynasty; on its own it only enables minor tricks until the character trains as a mage.
_Avoid_: Magic Skill (separate concept in loc)

**Magic Potential**:
Inherent aptitude in a character's blood that governs how quickly they gain mage levels and how fast mana regenerates once they are a trained mage.
_Avoid_: Magic Potency, Mage Potency

**Mage Level**:
An arbitrary but useful scale of a spell caster's capability; higher levels generally make spells more effective, with advancement rate tied to magic potential.

**School of Magic**:
A structured mental framework for a particular style of spellcasting — not a physical place. The six traditional schools are Chronomancy, Chiromancy, Elomancy, Necromancy, Biomancy, and Mensomancy.

**Chronomancy**:
The school focused on manipulating time — from minor personal effects to great feats such as arresting aging at mastery.

**Chiromancy**:
The school focused on life energy within living bodies, chiefly healing; related in tradition to Biomancy and Elomancy but distinct in use.

**Elomancy**:
The school focused on harnessing natural forces — fire, cold, electricity, golems, and destructive elemental effects.

**Necromancy**:
The school focused on entropy and decay, including undead creation and paths toward undeath.

**Biomancy**:
The school focused on altering living bodies — improvement or harm — rather than healing.

**Mensomancy**:
The school focused on manipulating minds, from mood shifts to complete domination at high mastery.

## Spells and casting

**Spell**:
A working produced by entering a trained state of mind and casting a spell construct into the world; distinguished from untrained "parlor tricks" by the mage trait and casting ability.

**Aura**:
A sustained spell — an ongoing frame of mind and monthly mana expenditure — as opposed to a one-shot effect.

**Mage Ritual**:
A complex spell requiring preparation, tools, multiple casters, or combinations thereof; often used for constructs (golems, undead) too elaborate for a single instant casting. Does not require ongoing maintenance like an aura once complete.
_Avoid_: Ritual spell (informal)

## World and dynasty

**Ley Line**:
A path of heightened mana concentration across the map; mages on a ley line can draw more power than elsewhere.
_Avoid_: Ley Lines (plural as term — use **Ley Line** as the concept name)

**Focal Point**:
A place where two or more ley lines intersect, greatly increasing ambient mana.
_Avoid_: Focal Points (plural as term)

**City of Magic**:
A domain where magic is woven into daily life — food, security, utilities, and administration — making the holding a wonder of arcane civilization.

**Magical Bloodline**:
A runic bloodline trait that alters the host at a cellular level — often improving strength, mana affinity, or other traits, and sometimes personality.
_Avoid_: Bloodline (alone — too generic in CK3)
